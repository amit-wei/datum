---
name: trade-study
description: Use when a design choice has more than one credible option and the requirements do not settle it: a stack, a store, where data lives, buy or build, a protocol. Compares options against the requirements that drive the choice and records the result. Not for choosing between needs or priorities (use grilling) or for comparing scenarios.
real: true
---

Name the drivers first: the requirements the choice serves (`drivers:`). List at least
two credible options, including the simplest. Score each against the drivers with
evidence (research, a spike), not taste. State what the winner costs and which
requirements it makes derived. A choice the drivers do not settle goes to the builder.
Output: a decision (`DEC-`) with the options inside it.
