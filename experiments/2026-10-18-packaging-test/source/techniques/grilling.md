---
name: grilling
description: Use when something only the builder can settle is open: a need, a preference, a priority, a premise, a missing value only they know, or a trade between goals; and once at the start of a new product. Asks pointed questions in batches. Not for facts that can be looked up (use research) or values that can be measured (use running-a-spike).
real: true
---

Ask in batches of about four. Each question states the issue, your recommended answer
and your confidence, so the builder can accept, reject or reframe it. Ask about what
the builder needs, not about solutions; a solution they name is a premise to probe.
Re-ask a skipped question briefly in the next batch. Stop when every open point is
settled or marked out of scope. Output: an elicitation record (`ELI-`) in the block's
`analysis/` folder, each need with the builder's words and the question that drew it
out; or, mid-step, the answer written into the field it unblocks.
